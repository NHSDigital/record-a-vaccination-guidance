Your list of patients allows you to see personal details for each patient alongside their eligibility status for RSV and pertussis. 

## Eligibility status tags  

There are three eligibility status tags: 

* Vaccinated 
* Eligible  
* Not yet eligible 

If you select ‘How eligibility status tags work’, you can see a key that explains what each status tag means. 

Status tags for RSV and pertussis update automatically if there are any changes. For example, if a person receives a vaccination, their status will update to ‘Vaccinated’.   

The number of ‘weeks pregnant’ will also automatically increase for each patient over time.  

To change who is on your list, you will need to upload a new CSV file. We cannot add or remove people from your list automatically.  

## Import issues 

You can select ‘Import issues’ to see patients on your list where we had issues matching their data. From there, you can decide whether you want to ignore those issues or upload a new CSV file and replace your list.
