Your list of patients allows you to see personal details for each patient alongside their eligibility status for RSV and pertussis. 

![Screen that shows a list of patients alongside their vaccination information](/images/maternity-images/patient-list.png)

## How patient data updates 

Patient data updates automatically overnight, but not in real time.  

For a patient’s eligibility status to change overnight, their vaccination must be recorded in RAVS or a GP system.  

As time passes, the number of ‘weeks pregnant’ will also update automatically for each patient.  

Patients are not automatically added to or removed from your list. To change who is on your list, you will need to upload a new CSV file.  

## Eligibility status tags  

There are three eligibility status tags: 

* Vaccinated 
* Eligible  
* Not yet eligible 

If you select ‘How eligibility status tags work’, you can see a key that explains what each status tag means. 

![Screen that shows a table where each status tag is defined](/images/maternity-images/status-tags.png)

## Import issues 

Select ‘Import issues’ to see any problems we’ve found with the imported patient data.  

![Screen that shows a list of patients who have issues with their information](/images/maternity-images/import-issues.png)

A patient will appear in ‘Import issues’ if: 

* they have no due date
* they have died 
* they have been flagged as sensitive
* their NHS number is superseded 

You can ignore the patients in your ‘Import issues’ list or upload a new CSV file to replace your list.
