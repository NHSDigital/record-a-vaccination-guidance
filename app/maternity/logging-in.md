To access this new Maternity area of RAVS, go to [NHS Record a vaccination](https://www.ravs.england.nhs.uk) and follow the [guidance for logging in to RAVS](https://guide.ravs.england.nhs.uk/logging-in/) as usual. 

Once you are logged in, select ‘Maternity’ from the menu.
