The patient upload history page gives you a full record of every CSV file that you and your team has uploaded to RAVS.

Your upload history helps you keep track of your patient lists and plan a regular upload routine. 

Each CSV file has 1 of 4 status tags: 

* Current file – your current list of patients
* Processed – a previous list of patients that has been replaced
* Processing – a CSV file currently being checked and matched
* Failed – a CSV file that did not successfully upload  

You can also see a file's name, the date and time it was uploaded, and who in your team uploaded it.
