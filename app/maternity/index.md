This guidance is for new area of RAVS that helps you manage vaccinations for patients who are pregnant.  

It is currently a private beta service, which means only a small group of trusts have access.  

For each patient, you can see:  

* name and NHS number 
* due date and number of weeks pregnant
* eligibility status for RSV and pertussis 

The service allows you to filter and sort patients in ways that help you make decisions about who to invite for vaccination.  

You can also export either a full or filtered list of patients.
