This guide covers the different sections of Record a vaccination (RAVS). 

You can also:

* [watch our training video](/training-video/) for an introduction to using RAVS
* [join a drop-in session](https://future.nhs.uk/vaccsandscreening/view?objectId=50814992), if you have any questions 

To access the service, go to [NHS Record a vaccination](https://www.ravs.england.nhs.uk).

## The vaccinations you can record in RAVS

Depending on your setting, you can record some or all of these vaccinations in RAVS:

- COVID-19
- Flu
- Pertussis
- RSV
- MenB
- MMR
- Pneumococcal

If you want to record one of these vaccinations but it is not available to you in RAVS, [contact your Regional Vaccination Operations Centre (RVOC)](https://future.nhs.uk/system/login?nextURL=%2Fconnect%2Eti%2Fvaccsandscreening%2Fview%3FobjectId%3D1004883).
