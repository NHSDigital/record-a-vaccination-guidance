This guidance describes what a group administrator can see and do in the Record a vaccination service (RAVS) as of mid-August. We will be adding more features in the coming months and will update this guidance as these become available. 

## Pharmacies section

The Pharmacies page lists pharmacies in your company that have already been onboarded to RAVS. 

The status of each pharmacy is shown as either active or invited. 

Active means at last 1 user has logged in to RAVS at that pharmacy. Invited means no one has logged in so far. 

### Viewing an individual pharmacy

Select **Manage** next to a pharmacy to see existing users at that pharmacy.

Users are shown as:

•	invited, if they have not yet logged in to RAVS
•	active, if they have logged in to RAVS
•	deactivated, if they have not logged in to RAVS for 90 days or have been deactivated by a lead administrator

If you do not have any invited or deactivated users, you only see active users.

You can also see each user's permission level and whether they are a vaccinator. 

This table shows the permission levels a user can have at an individual pharmacy. 


| **Permission level**       | **Record and edit vaccinations** | **Manage vaccines** | **Create reports** | **Manage users** |
|----------------------------|---------------------------|----------------------|-----------------|---|
| **Recorder**               | Yes                       | No                 | No             | No |
| **Administrator**          | Yes                       | Yes                  | Yes              | No |
| **Lead administrator**     | Yes                       | Yes                  | Yes              | Yes |


## Reports section

In Reports, you can create and download a report for your pharmacy chain that includes up to 90 days of data. 

You can choose:

•	different timeframes, including a date range of your choice
•	which vaccines to include
•	which pharmacies to include 
•	what level of detail to include

> [!NOTE]
> When you select pharmacies, the list you see only includes pharmacies that have added vaccine products in RAVS. Pharmacies that have not yet added any vaccine products cannot be included in a report.
